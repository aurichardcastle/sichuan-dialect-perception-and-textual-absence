### Key Analytical Findings

1. **Dialect density is extremely low.** Across 5 songs, only 9 confirmed Sichuan dialect features were found (2 UNCERTAIN). Three songs (花花公子, Coco Elva Tia) have ZERO dialect features. This confirms the research hypothesis that Masiwei is the most linguistically "standard" / English-oriented artist in the Chengdu group.

2. **Dialect features cluster in later-career songs.** The two albums from 2023 (狗咬狗: "P.Y.T" and "Babe") contain all 7 of the confirmed dialect features from those songs, while the 2020 and 2022 releases have zero. The 2025 song ("House Music") has 2 confirmed + 1 uncertain. This may suggest increasing dialect incorporation over time, but the sample is too small for statistical claims.

3. **"Babe" is the outlier.** With 5 confirmed dialect features, it accounts for over half of all Masiwei's dialect tokens in this corpus. Features include the locative 头 (grammar), 晓得 (pronunciation), 票儿/裤儿 (lexical), and the uncertain 担不懂.

4. **Zero Chengdu/Sichuan geographic or food references.** All geographic references are international aspirational locations (Paris, Pattaya, Sicily, Babylon). The single regional pride moment is the 巴适 line in "House Music." This is a stark contrast to what would be expected from other CDC members.

5. **English integration is the dominant non-Mandarin feature.** Masiwei's code-switching is Mandarin-English, not Mandarin-Sichuan. This makes him an important contrast case for studying how regional identity manifests (or doesn't) in Chinese hip-hop.

6. **No dialect-dependent rhymes.** Zero instances across all 5 songs where a rhyme scheme depends on Sichuan pronunciation. All rhymes work in standard Mandarin (or English).

7. **Thematic monotony.** All 5 songs are love/romance-themed. No songs primarily about regional pride, social commentary, cultural heritage, or community. This is analytically significant as a within-group contrast.
