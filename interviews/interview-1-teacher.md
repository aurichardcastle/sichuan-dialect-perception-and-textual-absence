### Interview 1: Participant 1 (Teacher)

**Context:** Chinese language teacher at Keats School, Chengdu. Interview conducted in Mandarin, July 2025. Participant is an active listener of Chengdu hip-hop.

**Q1: 你是怎么接触到嘻哈文化的？**
(How did you first encounter hip-hop culture?)

A: "中国有嘻哈"是一个中国的电视节目，在中国很流行，于是接触到了嘻哈文化。

Translation: "The Rap of China" is a Chinese TV show that was very popular in China, and that's how I encountered hip-hop culture.

**Q2: 你觉得成都的嘻哈氛围怎么样？**
(What do you think of Chengdu's hip-hop atmosphere?)

A: 我觉得成都的嘻哈氛围特别好，成都的嘻哈文化发展的很迅速，从成都集团的建立开始，歌曲的质量都很高，并且，四川话放在嘻哈歌曲里面也非常好听。

Translation: I think Chengdu's hip-hop atmosphere is particularly good. Chengdu's hip-hop culture has developed very rapidly. Since the establishment of the CDC, the quality of songs has been very high, and Sichuan dialect placed in hip-hop songs also sounds particularly good.

**Q3: 你最喜欢的成都嘻哈歌手是谁？为什么？**
(Who is your favorite Chengdu hip-hop artist? Why?)

A: 我最喜欢两位歌手，分别是王以太和KKECHO，因为他们的歌曲旋律都很好听，而且歌词写的很有意思，王以太的"别怕变老"是我最喜欢的嘻哈歌曲。

Translation: My two favorite artists are Wang Yitai and KKECHO, because their song melodies are very catchy and their lyrics are written in an interesting way. Wang Yitai's "Don't Be Afraid of Growing Old" is my favorite hip-hop song.

**Q4: 你觉得成都的嘻哈和其他城市有什么不同？**
(What do you think is different about Chengdu's hip-hop compared to other cities?)

A: 在中国嘻哈歌曲非常流行，其中成都和长沙的嘻哈歌曲我都很喜欢，但是成都的嘻哈歌曲节奏感更强一些，加上四川话会更加好听。

Translation: Hip-hop songs are very popular in China. I like both Chengdu's and Changsha's hip-hop songs, but Chengdu's hip-hop songs have a stronger sense of rhythm, and with Sichuan dialect added, they sound even better.

**Q5: 你参加过嘻哈音乐节或者演出吗？感觉怎么样？**
(Have you attended hip-hop festivals or performances? What was it like?)

A: 参加过，感觉特别好。现场的氛围很热烈，大家一起跟着节奏跳动，有一种很强的团体感。

Translation: Yes, I have. It felt particularly good. The live atmosphere was very lively, everyone moved to the rhythm together, and there was a strong sense of community.

**Q6: 你经常和谁一起听嘻哈音乐？**
(Who do you usually listen to hip-hop music with?)

A: 主要是和朋友们一起，偶尔也会自己听。

Translation: Mainly with friends, and occasionally I listen alone.

**Q7: 你更喜欢中国嘻哈还是外国嘻哈？为什么？**
(Do you prefer Chinese hip-hop or foreign hip-hop? Why?)

A: 我更喜欢中国的嘻哈，因为歌词更容易理解，而且有很多中国文化的元素在里面。

Translation: I prefer Chinese hip-hop, because the lyrics are easier to understand, and there are many elements of Chinese culture in them.

**Q8: 你觉得嘻哈歌词反映了真实生活吗？**
(Do you think hip-hop lyrics reflect real life?)

A: 是的，嘻哈歌词还是会反映真实生活的，尤其是一些社会问题。

Translation: Yes, hip-hop lyrics still reflect real life, especially some social issues.

**Q9: 嘻哈文化对年轻人有什么影响？**
(What impact does hip-hop culture have on young people?)

A: 嘻哈文化可以让年轻人更好地表达自己，也可以让他们关注社会问题，嘻哈可以批判现实和反映社会问题。

Translation: Hip-hop culture can help young people better express themselves and also get them to pay attention to social issues. Hip-hop can critique reality and reflect social problems.

**Q10: 你觉得中国嘻哈的未来发展怎么样？**
(How do you see the future development of Chinese hip-hop?)

A: 我觉得会越来越好，越来越多的歌手加入CDC，歌曲质量也在不断提高。

Translation: I think it will get better and better. More and more artists are joining the CDC, and song quality is continuously improving.

**Q11: 中外嘻哈文化对比，你有什么看法？**
(What are your views on comparing Chinese and Western hip-hop culture?)

A: 中国的嘻哈曾经也有一些不好的歌词或者互相diss，但是随着嘻哈文化的发展，越来越多的人喜欢嘻哈文化，所以在大环境的要求下就不会写不好的歌词，中国嘻哈歌曲更多的是传播中华文化，各个地域的特色，以及批判现实中的不好现象，例如：性别歧视、家庭暴力等。可以说目前的中国嘻哈都是相对来说比较正能量的。

Translation: Chinese hip-hop used to also have some negative lyrics or mutual dissing, but as hip-hop culture has matured and gained broader cultural acceptance, artists have naturally moved toward more constructive content. Chinese hip-hop songs today are more about spreading Chinese culture, the characteristics of each region, and addressing real social issues such as gender discrimination and domestic violence. It can be said that current Chinese hip-hop is relatively positive in its orientation.

中国嘻哈用地方方言来表达地域特色，而西方嘻哈则使用受纽约和洛杉矶影响的城市俚语。中国嘻哈更注重社会和谐和家庭价值观，而西方嘻哈则更关注个人奋斗和反抗权威。

Translation: Chinese hip-hop uses local dialects to express regional characteristics, while Western hip-hop uses urban slang influenced by New York and Los Angeles. Chinese hip-hop focuses more on social harmony and family values, while Western hip-hop focuses more on personal struggles and individualistic self-assertion.

**Q12: 你觉得成都嘻哈对你个人有什么意义？**
(What does Chengdu hip-hop mean to you personally?)

A: 成都嘻哈让我更加了解我所在城市的文化，也让我有了一种文化认同感。

Translation: Chengdu hip-hop has helped me better understand the culture of the city I live in, and it has given me a sense of cultural identity.
