### Interview 2: Participant 2 (Friend)

**Context:** Friend met during the Mandarin immersion program. Interview conducted in Mandarin, July 2025. Participant regularly attends hip-hop performances in Chengdu.

**Q1: 你是怎么接触到嘻哈文化的？**
(How did you first encounter hip-hop culture?)

A: 通过朋友和"中国有嘻哈"这个节目接触到了嘻哈文化。

Translation: Through friends and the show "The Rap of China."

**Q2: 你觉得成都的嘻哈氛围怎么样？**
(What do you think of Chengdu's hip-hop atmosphere?)

A: 成都的嘻哈氛围非常活泼而独特，特别是在街头文化方面。年轻人喜欢把四川话融入歌词中，给音乐增添了特别的味道。

Translation: Chengdu's hip-hop atmosphere is very lively and unique, especially in street culture. Young people love incorporating Sichuan dialect into lyrics, giving the music a special flavor.

**Q3: 你最喜欢的成都嘻哈歌手是谁？为什么？**
(Who is your favorite Chengdu hip-hop artist? Why?)

A: 我也最喜欢王以太和KKECHO。他们的歌词有故事性，旋律有冲击力，歌曲既能表达个人情感，又能触及社会问题。

Translation: My favorites are also Wang Yitai and KKECHO. Their lyrics are story-driven, melodies are impactful, and their songs can both express personal emotions and touch on social issues.

**Q4: 你觉得成都的嘻哈和其他城市有什么不同？**
(What do you think is different about Chengdu's hip-hop compared to other cities?)

A: 成都的嘻哈更注重本地特色，方言和地方文化让它与其他城市相比，独具地域风味。

Translation: Chengdu's hip-hop pays more attention to local characteristics. Dialect and regional culture make it stand out with unique regional flavors compared to other cities.

**Q5: 嘻哈音乐对你来说意味着什么？**
(What does hip-hop music mean to you?)

A: 音乐不仅是娱乐，它可以传递信息，激发情感。

Translation: Music is not just entertainment; it can convey messages and stir emotions.

**Q6: 你参加过现场演出吗？**
(Have you attended live performances?)

A: 经常参加，不仅是音乐演出，还有街头活动。

Translation: Frequently -- not only music performances but also street activities.

**Q7: 你更喜欢中国嘻哈还是外国嘻哈？为什么？**
(Do you prefer Chinese hip-hop or foreign hip-hop? Why?)

A: 我更喜欢中国嘻哈，因为它反映了中国独特的文化背景，语言的表达方式让我更容易产生共鸣。

Translation: I prefer Chinese hip-hop, because it reflects China's unique cultural background, and the way the language is expressed makes it easier for me to relate.

**Q8: 你觉得嘻哈歌词反映了真实生活吗？**
(Do you think hip-hop lyrics reflect real life?)

A: 是的，嘻哈歌词还是在讲述真实生活的故事，尤其是来自社会底层的声音。

Translation: Yes, hip-hop lyrics still tell real-life stories, especially voices from the lower strata of society.

**Q9: 嘻哈在批判和传播方面有什么作用？**
(What role does hip-hop play in terms of critique and dissemination?)

A: 嘻哈通过批判社会问题和揭露不公来反映社会现实，同时传播正面的社会能量。

Translation: Hip-hop reflects social reality by criticizing societal problems and exposing injustices, while also spreading positive social energy.

**Q10: 你觉得中国嘻哈的未来发展怎么样？**
(How do you see the future development of Chinese hip-hop?)

A: 中国嘻哈文化逐渐崛起，越来越多的歌曲关注社会问题和文化遗产，传达正能量和深刻的社会意义。

Translation: Chinese hip-hop culture has gradually risen. More and more songs are focusing on social issues and cultural heritage, conveying positive energy and deep social significance.

**Q11: 中外嘻哈文化对比，你有什么看法？**
(What are your views on comparing Chinese and Western hip-hop culture?)

A: 成都的嘻哈与社区身份和对本地文化的自豪感联系在一起，而西方嘻哈则更多关注个人经历和自我表达。

Translation: Chengdu's hip-hop is connected to community identity and pride in local culture, while Western hip-hop focuses more on individual experiences and self-expression.

**Q12: 成都嘻哈对你个人有什么影响？**
(What personal impact has Chengdu hip-hop had on you?)

A: 让我更加热爱这座城市，也更愿意去了解和传播本地文化。

Translation: It has made me love this city even more, and more willing to understand and spread local culture.
